ABOUT SCAMCHECK

Helping you make safer decisions online.

ScamCheck is a scam-awareness and screening platform designed to help users assess suspicious phone numbers, website links, and screenshots before deciding how to respond.

The platform combines internal scam records, external safety services, and AI-assisted analysis to provide an advisory risk assessment.

Try ScamCheck

HOW IT WORKS

How ScamCheck Works

A simple four-step process to help you assess suspicious content.

01

Submit

Enter a phone number or website URL, or upload a screenshot of suspicious content.

02

Check

ScamCheck checks available internal records, external safety services, or AI-assisted analysis depending on the type of scan.

03

Review

Review the returned risk level, suspicious indicators, explanation, and recommended action.

04

Decide

Use the information to make a more informed decision and independently verify suspicious requests where necessary.

OUR PURPOSE

Building scam awareness

ScamCheck aims to make scam screening easier to understand by bringing several checking methods into one platform.

The goal is not to replace independent verification, but to provide users with useful information before they respond to suspicious requests.

IMPORTANT REMINDER

A risk result is advisory

ScamCheck does not guarantee that content marked as LOW is safe, and a HIGH RISK result should still be considered alongside other available information.

Never provide passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, or payment information solely because a message appears legitimate.
